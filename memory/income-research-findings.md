## 3. Passive Income Streams (Investments/Royalties) ⭐⭐⭐

### Investment Opportunities

| Type | Entry Cost | Risk | Return |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| **Dividend Stocks** | $100+ | Medium | 3-5% annually |
| **REITs** | $100+ | Medium | 4-8% annually |
| **Music Royalties** | $500+ | High | Variable |
| **Peer-to-Peer Lending** | $100+ | Medium | 5-12% |
| **Licensing IP** | $0 (create first) | Low | 5-15% royalties |

### Music Royalties (Realistic)
- **Platform:** Royalty Exchange — buy shares in song royalties
- **Entry:** $500-5,000 for partial ownership
- **Return:** Depends on song performance; unpredictable
- **Reality Check:** Not truly passive — requires capital upfront

### Licensing Your Own IP
- Create digital products → license to others (PLR model)
- Create content → license for commercial use
- **Best path:** Build digital products first, then license excess

### Action Steps
1. Start with high-yield savings or index funds (simplest)
2. Consider small REITs (Fundrise, $10 minimum)
3. Build IP first (digital products), license later
4. Avoid music royalties unless you have capital to burn

## 4. Local Services (Austin Area) ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### Market Overview
Austin is booming — roofing, HVAC, home renovation in high demand after storms.

| Service | Startup Cost | Licensing? | Income Potential |
|---------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Roofing Sales/Lead Gen** | $0-500 | Not required for sales | $5,000-15,000/mo |
| **HVAC Maintenance** | $2,000+ | Yes (EPA 608) | $4,000-8,000/mo |
| **Pressure Washing** | $500-2,000 | No | $2,000-5,000/mo |
| **Landscaping** | $500-2,000 | No | $3,000-6,000/mo |
| **Handyman Services** | $200-1,000 | No | $3,000-7,000/mo |

### Roofing (Highest Opportunity)
- Austin roofers need leads constantly
- Can start as lead generator (no license needed)
- **Model:** Generate leads → sell to roofers ($50-200/lead)
- **Tool:** Twilio + Google Business to track leads

### Action Steps
1. **Quick win:** Lead generation for roofers (no credentials)
2. **Medium term:** Pressure washing or landscaping (low barrier)
3. **Long term:** Trade school + licensing for HVAC/electrical

## 5. E-commerce ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### Low-Investment Options

| Model | Startup Cost | Risk | Profit Margin |
|-------|-------------|------|---------------|
| **Print-on-Demand** | $0-100 | Very Low | 15-25% |
| **Dropshipping** | $0-500 | Low | 10-30% |
| **Reselling** | $200-1,000 | Medium | 20-50% |
| **Handmade Goods** | $100-500 | Low | 30-60% |

### Print-on-Demand (Safest)
- **Platforms:** Printful + Etsy or Shopify
- **Products:** T-shirts, mugs, posters — no inventory
- **Startup:** Free to start (Printful has free tier)
- **Niche focus essential:** Generic designs don't sell

### Etsy vs Shopify
- **Etsy:** Easier start, built-in traffic, higher fees (6.5%)
- **Shopify:** More control, lower fees, requires traffic building

### Action Steps
1. Start with Printful + Etsy (free, low risk)
2. Test 5-10 designs, double down on winners
3. Scale to Shopify when profitable
4. Avoid generic dropshipping — saturated
