# Autonomous Income Research - April 8, 2026

**5 Passive/Automated Income Opportunities:**

**1. AI Customer Support Bots (High ROI)**
- Deploy AI agents for small businesses (FAQ, lead qualification, booking)
- Charge: $300-1,500/month per client
- Setup: $50-200, then 1-2 hrs/month maintenance
- 10 clients = $3K-15K/mo recurring
- Best niches: real estate, dental, e-commerce

**2. Print-on-Demand (True Passive)**
- Etsy + Printify - design once, sell forever
- Low upfront, no inventory, auto-fulfill
- Potential: $500-2K/mo with good designs
- Trending: motivational, niche hobbies, pet-themed

**3. AI Content Pipelines**
- Automated content (social, blog, email) for businesses
- Retainers: $500-2K/mo, 30 min/week QA per client
- 20-30 clients = full salary

**4. AI Lead Generation Agents**
- Monitor FB groups, reviews, forums for intent
- Charge: $50-200/lead OR $1K-3K/mo retainer
- High value = low churn

**5. Digital Products (Evergreen)**
- E-books, templates, Notion dashboards, Canva templates
- Platform: Gumroad, Etsy
- 80%+ margin after creation

**Recommendations:**
- Quickest start: Print-on-Demand
- Highest ceiling: AI lead gen / support bots
- Most passive: Digital products + POD
