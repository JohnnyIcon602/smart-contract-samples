## 3. Austin Roofing Lead Gen — Updated

### Current Market (April 2026)
- Austin still in storm season — high demand
- Lead gen rates: **$50–$200/lead** depending on quality
- Common model: You generate leads → roofer pays per lead

### How to Start
1. Set up Google Business profile for lead tracking
2. Create simple landing page with Twilio forwarding
3. Run local Facebook/Google ads ($200–500/mo budget)
4. Package and sell leads to 3–5 roofers

### Tools Needed
- Twilio: ~$1/call (already configured in TOOLS.md)
- Google Business: Free
- Simple landing page: Free (Canva or Carrd)

### Realistic Earnings
- **Month 1:** $0–500 (learning + building reputation)
- **Month 2–3:** $1,000–3,000/mo
- **Month 6+:** $3,000–8,000/mo with consistent leads
