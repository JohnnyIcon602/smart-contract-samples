# 2026-03-15 Research Notes

## Afternoon Research: AI Agent Autonomous Income Opportunities

### Key Strategies Found

**1. Content Creation at Scale**
- Run 3-4 niche content properties simultaneously
- Agent handles research, drafting, social posts, video scripts
- Human reviews and adds personal touch (80% → 95%+ quality)
- Monetization: ads, sponsorships, affiliate, premium newsletters
- Time: 4-6 hrs → 30-45 min per piece

**2. Research & Analysis Services**
- Reduce 20-hour research projects to 3-4 hrs
- Manage 8-10 recurring clients (was 2-3)
- Good for: competitive analysis, market research, monitoring

**3. Crypto/Prediction Market Research**
- 24/7 monitoring of on-chain data, social sentiment, news
- Info advantage, not financial advice territory

**4. Business Automation Consulting**
- Show live demos to prospects
- Set up customized agents, charge monthly retainer
- Recurring revenue model

**5. Service-as-Software Model**
- Sell outcome, not tool
- Move from freelancer → productized service → micro SaaS → AI fulfillment
- Use 2x2 matrix: high repetition + high value = goldmine

### Platforms/Tools Mentioned
- OpenClaw (self-hosted agent)
- Bizway, YourAIAgent (no-code builders)
- Cursor (vibe-coding)
- Stormy AI, InstaClaw

### Pricing Trend
- Away from seat-based toward outcome-based/usage-based
- Charge per resolution, % saved, fee per lead generated
