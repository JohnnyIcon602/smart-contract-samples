### Key Insights from Latest Research

**AI Customer Support Bots:**
- Monthly charge: $300-1,500/client
- Niche focus: Real estate, dental, e-commerce
- Setup cost only $50-200 (hosting + API)
- 1-2 hours/month maintenance after setup
- **ROI**: A human support agent costs $3K-4K/mo; AI is fraction of that

**Content Pipelines:**
- Retainers: $500-2,000/mo per client
- 30 min/week actual time per client
- Scalable to 20-30 clients before needing help
- Tools: AI content generation + scheduling (Postiz, etc.)

**Lead Generation (Highest Leverage):**
- Performance-based: $50-200 per qualified lead
- Retainer: $1K-3K/mo for full system
- Hybrid: $500/mo base + per-lead bonus
- Perfect for: plumbers, personal injury lawyers, HVAC

**Data Monitoring (Most Passive):**
- Subscription: $200-1,000/mo per client
- Setup fee: $500-2,000 one-time
- Near-zero maintenance after config
- Very low churn - "data becomes addictive"
