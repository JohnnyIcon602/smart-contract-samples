## Evening Research: Autonomous Income Opportunities

**1. AI-Powered Newsletter (Beehiiv)**
- Build once, earn forever — weekly email to 1K+ subscribers
- Auto-sponsor matching via Beehiiv ad network: $500–$1,500/mo potential
- Cost: $0–$99/mo | Breakeven: 60–120 days
- Best for: Finance, tech, crypto niches

**2. Faceless YouTube with AI Voiceover (ElevenLabs)**
- Script + AI voice = automated video production
- One creator hitting $4,200/mo with 3 hrs/week effort
- Cost: $5–$99/mo | Tools: ElevenLabs + CapCut/Descript
- Niche: News recaps, history, self-help compilations

**3. Automation Services (Make.com)**
- Build workflows for small businesses — they pay $200–$500/mo for maintenance
- 5 recurring clients = $1,000–$2,500/mo semi-passive
- Cost: $0–$19/mo | Highest ROI if you learn the platform

**4. Digital Products (AI-Assisted)**
- Templates, ebooks, calculators, Notion systems
- Sell on Gumroad/Teachable — zero marginal cost per sale
- AI tools (Claude/ChatGPT) cut creation time 5x
- Examples: Resume templates, budget spreadsheets, startup kits

**5. Print-on-Demand with AI Art (Midjourney)**
- Generate designs → upload to Redbubble/Merch by Amazon
- Cost: $10–$30/mo for Midjourney
- Income: $200–$1,500/mo per design (compounds over time)

---

*Bottom line:* Newsletter and YouTube have the best true-passive potential with lowest upfront effort. Automation services pay faster but require client management. Pick one and go deep.
