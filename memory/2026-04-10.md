### Research Results: Autonomous Income Opportunities

| Skill/Platform | What It Does | Money Potential |
|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| AI Customer Support Bots | Deploy AI agents for small businesses (real estate, dental, e-commerce) handling FAQs, lead qualification, appointment booking. Monthly management fee. | $300-1,500/client/month. 10 clients = $3k-15k/mo recurring |
| Automated Content Pipelines | Build AI systems that generate/schedule posts across social media, blogs, email newsletters. Handle heavy lifting, human does quality control. | $500-2,000/client/month. 20-30 clients = full-time salary |
| AI Lead Generation Agents | Monitor online conversations for intent, flag opportunities, qualify inbound leads, book appointments automatically. | $50-200/qualified lead OR $1k-3k/mo retainer |
| AI Data Monitoring/Alert Systems | Custom intelligence feeds - track competitor pricing, brand mentions, customer sentiment, regulatory changes. | $200-1,000/client/month + $500-2k setup |

### Key Insights
- All models are recurring revenue (subscription-based)
- Low time investment after initial setup (1-2 hrs/month)
- No capital needed - just AI tools and automation skills
- Highest ROI: Lead generation (tied directly to client revenue)
