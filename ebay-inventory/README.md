## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Image URLs

eBay requires hosted image URLs. Google Drive links won't work directly.

## Solution

**Option 1: Upload CSV without photos (Recommended)**
1. Upload CSV to eBay
2. eBay creates "draft" listings
3. Add photos manually in eBay

**Option 2: Host images elsewhere**
1. Download photos from Google Drive
2. Upload to imgur.com or postimages.org  
3. Replace the URLs in CSV

---

## How to Upload to eBay

1. Go to: **eBay Seller Hub → Reports → Upload**
2. Select: **Listings template**
3. Download the CSV I created
4. Upload it to eBay
5. Add photos to drafts
