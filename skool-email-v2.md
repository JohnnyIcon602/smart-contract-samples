## Email 2: The Financing Secret

**Subject:** How I bought 9 businesses with $0 down

**Body:**

Banks are the slow way to buy a business.

Here's what actually works:

**Seller financing:**
Most sellers would rather get paid over time than lose their business to a stranger. I've negotiated 30-50% down payments stretched over 3-5 years.

**Earnouts:**
You pay based on performance. If the business grows, you pay more. If it doesn't, you pay less. Aligned incentives.

**ROI financing:**
Use the business's own cash flow to pay for itself. Buy a company that makes $4k/month? Your payment is $2k. The rest is profit.

These 3 methods got me 9 businesses without touching my own cash.
